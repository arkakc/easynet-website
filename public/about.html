Company Overview

Who We Are

Easynet IT Solutions Limited is a 100% Papua New Guinea-owned IT consulting, sales and services company. We focus on IT hardware and software sales, IT infrastructure and networking, server, cloud and cybersecurity solutions, ERP and business software implementation, and technical support and managed IT services. From our Port Moresby head office we serve clients in Lae, Mount Hagen, Goroka, Madang, Kokopo, Wewak and every province in Papua New Guinea — including mining and resources, agriculture and fisheries, banking and finance, retail, logistics and transport, construction, hotels, schools and NGOs.

Easynet operates its own website, social media pages, branding, company email and marketing activities — and takes full responsibility for every customer relationship, from the first conversation through to delivery and ongoing support.

Our service philosophy: we start with your business problem, recommend the technology that solves it, deliver it locally, and stay with you through ongoing support — so technology becomes an advantage, not a cost.

Our Vision

To become the trusted technology partner for PNG businesses by delivering reliable, innovative and practical IT solutions.

Our Mission

To help Papua New Guinea businesses grow by providing complete technology solutions — from online presence and lead generation to business automation, secure IT infrastructure and dependable ongoing support.
