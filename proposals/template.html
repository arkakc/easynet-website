Executive summary

One private network for your whole business

This proposal sets out how Easynet IT Solutions will connect your head office and every branch, warehouse, mine site or retail outlet into one private, encrypted network — using Starlink satellite internet wrapped in enterprise SD-WAN technology. 01 · The challenge

Why connecting branches in PNG is harder — and more expensive — than it should be

Most PNG businesses with more than one location connect them the 2005 way: telco MPLS circuits. Each branch rents a small leased line (commonly 2–5 Mbps) that is carried over long-distance backhaul to Port Moresby. It works — until you look closely at what you are actually paying for.

FIG 1 The traditional model: every branch rents a small, expensive circuit with a single point of failure.

Paying premium prices for small pipes

A leased MPLS circuit costs like a premium product but often delivers a few congested megabits. You pay the same monthly fee on good days and bad days.

Growth waits on the telco

Opening a new branch means ordering a circuit and waiting weeks or months. Meanwhile the new site runs on paperwork and patience, not live systems.

Outages hit the P&L directly

When the circuit drops, the branch can't sell, can't sync stock, can't process cards. Every silent hour is unrecorded revenue and idle staff.

No visibility, no control

When something is slow, nobody can say whether it's the branch, the backhaul or the head office. Problems become blame, and blame becomes downtime.

Remote sites simply can't get it

Mine sites, farms, islands and rural stations outside the fibre footprint are offered nothing at all — or a microwave link that fades in weather.

“Backup” usually means a phone call

Without automatic failover, an outage becomes a 2 a.m. phone call and a manual reconfiguration — if anyone knows how.

02 · The solution

Starlink + SD-WAN: a private MPLS-grade network, built in days

In plain English: the public internet is a highway open to everyone. SD-WAN (Software-Defined Wide Area Networking) builds a private, armoured lane on that highway — reserved for your traffic, encrypted end-to-end, with a traffic controller that decides which of your applications goes first. Starlink provides the on-ramp: high-capacity satellite internet available at any site in PNG. Your existing MPLS or 4G circuits are not ripped out — they are kept and managed as extra paths. Complement, not replace.

FIG 2 Hub-and-spoke: every site — city or remote — joins the same private network through its own Starlink path.

Anywhere in PNG

If a site can see the sky, it can join your private network — islands, highlands, mine sites, farms, rural stations.

MPLS-grade behaviour

Encrypted tunnels, application priority and steady performance — the properties you were promised by MPLS, delivered over satellite.

Live in days

A dish and an edge appliance are installed and commissioned in a site visit — no civil works, no telco lead times.

03 · Resilience

When a circuit fails, your business doesn't notice

Resilience is not bought off the shelf — it is engineered. Every site runs at least two independent paths (Starlink plus your existing MPLS or a 4G backup). The SD-WAN controller probes every path continuously, duplicates your most critical packets across both, and shifts traffic the moment a path degrades. No leased-line dependency, no human in the loop.

Appendix

Plain-English glossary

TermWhat it means, without the jargon
SD-WANSoftware that turns any internet connection into a smart, private corporate network link — choosing the best path for every application, automatically.
MPLSThe traditional private circuit product sold by telcos for ~20 years. Reliable where it exists, but expensive, small and slow to order in PNG.
Starlink (LEO)Internet from a constellation of low-orbit satellites. High capacity and low delay compared with old satellite internet; available almost anywhere.
Hub & spokeThe shape of your network: head office is the hub; every branch is a spoke with its own secure link home.
Encrypted tunnelYour data is sealed in a private envelope before it touches any public network, and unsealed only at your end. Nobody in between can read it.
Application steering / QoSThe network's traffic controller: your ERP and phone calls jump the queue; YouTube and big downloads wait their turn.
FailoverThe automatic switch to a backup path when the main path misbehaves. In this design it happens in seconds, with nobody touching anything.
Latency & packet lossDelay and dropped data — the two invisible causes of “the system is slow”. SD-WAN measures both constantly and routes around trouble.
NOCNetwork Operations Centre — Easynet's monitoring desk in Port Moresby that watches every site and reports to you monthly.
Edge applianceThe small hardened box we install at each site — firewall, encryption and steering in one unit, pre-configured before it arrives.

Our promise in one sentence: we design it, build it, secure it, monitor it and answer the phone when you call — one PNG company, accountable for your whole network.
